# What survives the sensitivity analysis

The limited central conclusion survives: comparisons depend on which sources,
weights and temporal supports enter the measurement. Matching populations
usually narrows the tested disagreement, but does not provide universal
reconciliation, physical assignment truth or a molecular-accuracy certificate.

All eight acquisitions / nine views and all 81 specified settings completed.
Every original-setting source/weight/moment replay and independent retained
arithmetic recount passed. The historical seven-of-nine result remains intact.

- Matched fit gaps narrow in 68/81 settings and worsen in 13/81. Five views
  narrow in all nine settings: EDF10 replicates 1–2, RTX replicates 1 and 3,
  and raw SDC. These are five views, not five independent laboratories.
- NPC and Figure4 untreated each narrow in only four settings. RTX replicate
  2 narrows in eight, although its original setting worsens. The two original
  worsening views are not a stable biological class.
- EDF10 replicate 2 retains positive code-minus-diagonal pointwise intervals
  at q=.15 in all nine settings, but at q=.10 in only four. The original
  two-frequency disagreement is not uniformly supported across selection.
  These are conditional, unadjusted sensitivity intervals, not nine replications.
- Both frequencies have usable selected-code/diagonal Fieller sets in 78/81
  arms. The other three SDC arms retain twelve all-real/disjoint sets across
  the two methods and two frequencies. A finite point fit, or a narrow paired
  difference, does not repair unsupported individual measurements.
- Aggressive source selection can produce apparent perfect agreement by
  leaving singleton coarse groups. The code/diagonal identity is exact there,
  not empirical validation. After removing such groups from all three
  contrasts, the post-hoc diagnostic narrows 65/77 supported comparisons,
  worsens twelve and leaves four unsupported. Primary results are unchanged.
- Reference-response coefficients replay for all six CD20 preparations.
  All 36 noise-model/weight transformations include finite observation and
  coefficient uncertainty via conservative rectangles. Some intervals are
  broad: EDF10 replicate 1's localization-weight response spans roughly
  1.1–37.3 nm² across the two model intervals. This is limited precision,
  not a tight calibration. The old qualification outcomes (4/6 event-weighted,
  2/6 localization-weighted) do not improve merely because uncertainty was added.

The repaired scientific presentation should say that reference restriction
**often changes and narrows comparisons, with preparation/selection-dependent
residual disagreement**. It should not claim a general correction, equivalence,
or validation of discarded molecules. The selected-row coverage range across
the sensitivity is 0.1–61.1% of code inputs, not all raw localizations.

The additional response sets remain conditional on inferred templates, original
QC and the two noise laws; template and physical source uncertainty are not
covered. Binding-event formation, raw precision filters and temporal panels
remain fixed. The analysis strengthens robustness characterization and reveals
an algebraic agreement trap. It does not supply a new physical experiment,
independent confirmation, safe selector, superiority result or predictable
Nature Methods acceptance. D076 remains unrun and cannot validate the proposed
randomized temporal intervention.
