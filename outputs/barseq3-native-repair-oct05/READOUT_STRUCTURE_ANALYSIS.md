# Pre-outcome interpretation and related-work boundaries

This document is fixed before any BARseq3 perturbed recovery decode. It does not
change the frozen policies, endpoint, threshold, crop or acceptance gates.

The potential comparative conclusion is narrow: equal marginal geometric-error
distributions need not imply equal molecular-output recovery. The allocation
of those errors across readouts and the decoder family can matter. An actual
author barcode-model optimizer is included; matching its performance would
support this comparative conclusion without establishing our unique superiority.

Mechanistic prediction: separated camera groups leave each spatial locus with
only part of a codeword. Under the explicitly restricted-support assumption,
the correlation evidence bound sqrt(k/10) predicts vulnerability by camera
balance. For the BARseq3 book, define *balanced* sequences as having at most six
readouts in either G/C versus T/A group; all other sequences are *unbalanced*.
The fixed correlation threshold .775 requires seven supported on-readouts even
at infinite signal. We will retain native-reference counts, identity-consistent
matched calls and lost reference calls by these two groups for every policy,
field, decoder, condition and matching radius. These are reference-consistency
diagnostics, not true biological sensitivity or FDR. Grouping is based only on
the public codebook, never selected by a favorable outcome. The headline
comparison remains the originally frozen all-call gene-class identity F1.

Displacement follows native unmixing/conditioning. This isolates an idealized
residual mode; it is not a raw-camera production experiment. Native algorithm
outputs are not independent RNA truth, and two fields on one BARseq3 preparation
do not create two preparations. Annotated CSHL data supply a different reference
endpoint on a different release; their two crop names alone do not establish
two independent animals.

Matching a marginal RMS/covariance does **not** match the paper's temporal
contrast T. The common-displacement model has explicit code-exogeneity and
stability assumptions; a fixed camera-group offset can violate that model and
has a different temporal structure. This study neither runs the randomized
contrast nor demonstrates equal measured T with different molecular accuracy.
It tests a separate limitation of reducing readout-resolved geometry to a
marginal scalar budget. Do not describe its success as a validated bridge from
the conditional temporal estimator to RNA accuracy.

The existence of barcode-dependent error and imperfect blank proxies is already
known. The Moffitt laboratory's public [MERFISH data-analysis
protocol](https://www.protocols.io/view/rna-imaging-with-merfish-data-analysis-metc3en.pdf)
discusses how codeword relationships and unequal bit errors affect confidence;
its confidence ratio is qualitative. [Luce et al., Scientific Reports
2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17477-1) report reproducible readout-dependent
blank variation and non-specific readout binding. We must not claim that
"blanks are imperfect" or "color registration matters" is a new discovery.
Our selected actual-kernel counterexample is a concrete limitation, not evidence
that those authors' empirical blank-control results are invalid.

Material new evidence would need the frozen complete comparison to show a
consequential difference at matched full error distributions, survive strong
alternatives, and connect the observed difference to a declared readout/decoder
mechanism on the external public preparation. A harmful zero-case correction,
failed gate, weak group prediction or author-model tie remains visible. No
outcome warrants a claim of guaranteed Nature Methods acceptance or validates
the conditional randomized temporal intervention.
