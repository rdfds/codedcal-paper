The bounded codebook search restores the native conditional catalog under imposed
color shifts in the public STARfinder culture example. A stronger conventional
multistart template also restores it. The initial large advantage therefore does
not survive the strongest comparator on this source.

That initial comparison is now superseded by stronger barcode-model starts and
a distinct public tissue attempt. Strong author refinement raises BARseq3 field
zero color-case F1 to 0.971/0.979 (correlation/regression), rather than 0.791/0.611.
In field one, the older author policy remains the strongest tested conventional
result at the same three-pixel radius, 0.0294/0.0086; the new loss-selected starts give only 0.015/0.000.
Search recovery remains exact there. A wide residual difference is real on that
exposed field, but it does not establish a preparation-general decision rule.
The fresh frozen tissue workflow failed native eligibility. Its source-informed
repair and all subsequent tissue comparisons are post-hoc development.

The historical BARseq3 primary report uses radius five (.0588/.0115 for that
older author policy). New diagnostics here report radius three, with all three
radii retained; these must not be compared as if the radii were identical.

## What this establishes and leaves unresolved

The useful supported development conclusion is conditional: calibration search
and initialization can materially alter recovery of the same native molecular
catalog, despite identical injected displacement distributions. Better starts
remove some apparent superiority. The restricted search can recover cases where
tested conventional optimization fails, but can itself fail severely outside
its class. Both search and the strong author optimizer already receive the same
physical codebook: this comparison changes optimization and prior restrictions,
not an independently measured new observable. A search win alone therefore cannot
prove that conventional measurements are insufficient or validate temporal T. Low absent-code counts and low pixel losses can conceal these output
changes. All statements concern controlled output consistency, not natural RNA
truth or physical temporal validation.

The large remaining search advantage is confined to one exposed BARseq3 field;
it is not a replicated five-point advantage across preparations. This record
does **not** yet establish a frozen, independently reproduced
recommendation across preparations, or a decisive exceptional Nature Methods
advantage. Fresh independent testing must include the corrected full-field
adapter, strong starts and safe abstentions; it cannot reuse the failed frozen
study as a positive confirmation. Other culture/tissue fields, reserved kidney
fields, D076 images and compartment markers remain unopened. Failed S3/ISS,
fiducial-density conditions and simulation-only randomized MERFISH remain.
