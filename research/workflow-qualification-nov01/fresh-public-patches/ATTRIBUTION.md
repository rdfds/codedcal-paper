# Public source patches

Tsui, Jenkin, Naila Adam, Cristina Flores, Shadi Ansari, Esther Kong, Yukta Thapliyal, Ciara O'Flanagan, Dario Bressan, IMAXT Cancer Grand Challenges Consortium, Samuel Aparicio, Andrew Roth. *MERFISH 4T1 Cell-Culture Raw Unaligned Images*, Harvard Dataverse, DOI10.7910/DVN/NE0WOP, released version1. [Source](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/NE0WOP). [CC BY4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

These are extracted central256x256 cells, native z-index7 RNA readouts plus published frame6 fiducials, field000. They are transposed and flipped on both axes according to the microscope metadata; source TIFFs are not redistributed here. Full original TIFF MD5/SHA256, native frame indices and extraction SHA256 are recorded in ../fresh-collection-receipts.json and ../fresh-input.json. Image outcomes have only been examined for native qualification, not comparative confirmation.
