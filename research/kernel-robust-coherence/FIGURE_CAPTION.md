# Research summary, separate from manuscript displays

A. Exact population example with two equal-weight sources and window-B kernel
phases +/-phi. The bound is G=|m|cos(phi); dotted lines give true attenuation.
The dashed .96 line is the fixed new method-development threshold. Reduced G
does not identify motion, since phase-kernel heterogeneity alone lowers it.

B. All 279 exposed calibration sources in 139 groups, common numerical count
scale, same four-count weights and total diagonal signal. Exact conditional code
variance and a calibration group-scatter proxy for a calibration-fitted witness.
The logarithmic scale shows a real conditional design reduction. These bars
are not held-out physical error budgets or confidence coverage results.

C. Favorable model-based controls with stable kernels and actual |m|=1.
Bars report the fixed finite-mixture g=.96 test call fraction. Every method
abstains at 139 groups; at 4096 synthetic groups, H4 still abstains while
source-resolved and independently design-flagged alternatives succeed. These
alternatives require additional information. No additional independent real
groups were collected. The complete ten-scenario results, including failed
assumptions and approximate methods, remain in simulation-results.json.
